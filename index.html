Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions About HVAC Service in Troy MI

Do you offer 24/7 emergency furnace or AC repair in Troy?

Yes. We understand that heating and cooling problems don’t just occur during business hours. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week and will get a professional technician to your home or business quickly.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Troy MI and the Metro Detroit area for both residential and commercial heating and cooling needs. We handle most of the Oakland County market and surrounding areas.

Do you service all brands and models?

Yes. Our trained professionals service all makes and models of furnaces and air conditioners. We work with most major manufacturers and use only top brand-name products for new installations.

How do I know if I need furnace repair or a full replacement?

The average life of a furnace is 15–20 years. Our specialists will come out, troubleshoot the problem, and give you all the information you need to make an informed decision — repair or replace — with no pressure.

Do you offer financing for a new furnace or AC?

Yes. We have financing plans available that greatly reduce the upfront cost of a new furnace or air conditioner and spread it out over a longer period of time. We also provide free quotes on every new installation.

How quickly can you come out for a furnace or AC repair?

We aim to get to you as quickly as possible — often the same day or within hours of your call. For emergency situations involving heating or cooling failure, we are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including weekends and holidays.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Troy Furnace and Air Conditioning is fully licensed and insured in Michigan. All of our technicians are trained professionals held to the highest standards in the HVAC industry.
